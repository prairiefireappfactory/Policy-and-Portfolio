Micro Radar Privacy Policy

Effective 11 September 2026. Developer: Prairie Fire App Factory.

Who we are

This policy applies to the Android app Micro Radar, published by Prairie Fire App Factory (“we”, “us”). Contact: prairiefireappfactory@gmail.com.

Data the app uses

The app asks for permission to use your device’s location to show nearby aircraft. It also uses the internet to request live aircraft data from the OpenSky Network (opensky-network.org). That request includes the map area being viewed, which is based on your location, and it is made from your device directly to OpenSky. We do not operate a server, and we do not receive or store your location. Location is also provided by Google Play services on your device. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. You can refuse or withdraw the location permission at any time in Android Settings; the app will then show aircraft only for an area you choose, if at all.

Who we share data with

The only third parties involved are the OpenSky Network, which receives the aircraft data request described above (see its own privacy policy), and Google Play services, which supplies device location under Google’s own terms. We do not sell data and we do not share it with anyone else.

How data is protected

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to store or protect on a server. Anything the app keeps stays inside the app’s private storage on your device and is protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device lock.

How long data is kept

We keep no data. Anything saved on your device stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has stored by opening Android Settings, choosing Apps, selecting the app, and tapping Storage, then Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing further to request from us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
