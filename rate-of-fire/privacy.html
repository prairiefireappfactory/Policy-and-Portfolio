Rate of Fire Privacy Policy

Effective 11 September 2026. Developer: Prairie Fire App Factory.

Who we are

This policy applies to the Android app Rate of Fire, published by Prairie Fire App Factory (“we”, “us”). Contact: prairiefireappfactory@gmail.com.

Data the app uses

The app asks for permission to use your device’s microphone so it can listen to sound while you use it. Audio is processed on your device in real time. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. You can refuse or withdraw the microphone permission at any time in Android Settings; the listening feature will then not work.

Who we share data with

We do not share audio or any other data with anyone, because we do not receive it.

How data is protected

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to store or protect on a server. Anything the app keeps stays inside the app’s private storage on your device and is protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device lock.

How long data is kept

We keep no data. Anything saved on your device stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has stored by opening Android Settings, choosing Apps, selecting the app, and tapping Storage, then Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing further to request from us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
